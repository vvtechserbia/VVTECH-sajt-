About

VVTECH is an engineering company based in Pančevo, Serbia. We develop complete systems and individual subassemblies — from requirements definition and analysis, through prototyping and testing, to the technical documentation required for series production.

We cover guidance and control, flight dynamics, mechanical design, electronics and firmware, RF and telemetry, software, testing and documentation. In addition to development services, we design and build our own telemetry and communication devices.

We work with companies that develop, integrate or manufacture technical systems, in Serbia and abroad.

How we work

Work is divided into phases. For each phase the input, the deliverable and the acceptance criterion are agreed in advance.

PhaseInputDeliverable
1. RequirementsThe customer's specification or a description of the problemScope document with acceptance criteria
2. AnalysisRequirements, available data, constraintsModel, analysis results, report stating the assumptions
3. PrototypeThe agreed designFunctional unit, source code, schematics and drawings
4. TestingPrototype and test planMeasurement records and test report
5. HandoverAccepted resultsTechnical documentation for production, training

Forms of cooperation

On a project

  • Development on request. We take over a complete system or a clearly defined subassembly, with agreed requirements, deadlines and documentation.
  • Project cooperation. We work on part of the scope in the customer's project, as an extension of its development team.
  • Independent review. Review of an existing design, a feasibility study, a technical specification or the technical part of a bid.

Permanent capacity in Serbia

  • Dedicated engineering centre. Establishment and operation of a development office in Serbia for the partner's needs.
  • Joint company. A jointly owned entity, branch or representative office in Serbia.
  • Partner investment. The partner invests in equipment, laboratory and staff for long-term cooperation; we contribute the engineering work and know-how.

The ownership structure, rights to results and the investment model are agreed in advance and in writing.

Confidentiality

We sign a non-disclosure agreement before any technical data is exchanged. We do not publish customers' data, drawings or results, and we do not name customers as references without written consent.
