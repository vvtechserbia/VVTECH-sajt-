About

VVTECH is an engineering company based in Pančevo, Serbia. We develop complete systems and individual subassemblies: we define requirements, carry out analysis and simulation, build prototypes, run tests and prepare the technical documentation for production.

In addition to development on request, we design and build our own telemetry and communication devices, and the software for displaying and analysing field data.

We work with companies that develop, integrate or manufacture technical systems, in Serbia and abroad. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before any technical data is exchanged.

Capabilities

We take on a complete system or a single subassembly, within a scope agreed in advance.

Guidance, navigation and control

Guidance laws, autopilot, estimation and sensor fusion, GNSS/INS integration, verification in simulation and on hardware.

Flight dynamics and ballistics

Aerodynamic characterisation, 6 and 7 DOF trajectory simulation, stability, dispersion.

RF, telemetry and communications

Radio links and link budget, antennas, telemetry chain, ground station and data transfer.

Electronics and firmware

Schematics and PCB, power supplies, sensor and actuator interfaces, firmware, data recording.

Mechanical design

Assemblies and housings, strength calculations, production drawings, functional prototypes.

Software

Simulation and measurement processing tools, operator applications, display and reporting.

Testing

Test matrices and procedures, instrumentation, laboratory and field testing, test reports.

Integration and documentation

Integration of a subassembly into an existing system, interface definition, documentation for series production.
