Services

We cover the whole chain — mechanics, electronics, software and cloud — so your solution doesn't have to be assembled from multiple vendors.

Industrial automation

Automation of processes and machinery: control cabinets, microcontroller and PLC control, sensors and actuators, safety functions, as well as modernization (retrofit) of existing plants.

Custom embedded systems

Development of purpose-built electronics and firmware: microcontroller platforms, GSM/LTE and GPS telemetry, industrial protocols, energy-efficient and solar-powered designs, over-the-air (OTA) updates in the field.

Industrial equipment & machines

Design and manufacturing of purpose-built machines and devices for specific production needs — from surface-treatment machinery to special field equipment. From concept to commissioning.

Cloud software solutions

IoT platforms for remote monitoring and control of devices: server infrastructure, web and mobile applications, real-time notifications and alarms, data history and analytics.

How we work

A clear process, no surprises — at every step you know where the project stands.

  1. 01

    Consulting & analysis

    We get to know your problem, process and operating conditions. We define requirements, scope and budget.

  3. 03

About us
