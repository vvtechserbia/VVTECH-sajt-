Capabilities

The disciplines we cover with our own engineering capacity. We take on a complete system or a single subassembly.

Guidance, navigation and control

Development of guidance and control algorithms and their verification before integration.

  • guidance laws and autopilot design
  • state estimation and sensor fusion
  • GNSS/INS integration
  • inertial sensor calibration
  • verification in simulation (SIL) and with hardware in the loop (HIL)

Aerodynamics and flight dynamics

Analysis and simulation describing the motion of a system and assessing its behaviour.

  • aerodynamic characterisation
  • 6 and 7 DOF trajectory simulation
  • ballistics and stability analysis
  • dispersion and Monte Carlo studies

Mechanical design

Design of assemblies and subassemblies, from concept to production drawings.

  • housings, brackets and mechanisms
  • strength calculations
  • material and tolerance selection
  • production drawings
  • functional prototypes

Electronics and firmware

Purpose-built electronics and the software that runs on it.

  • schematic and PCB design
  • power supplies
  • sensor and actuator interfaces
  • firmware for microcontroller platforms
  • on-board data acquisition and recording

RF, telemetry and communications

Data transfer between a system and ground equipment.

  • radio links and link budget
  • antennas
  • telemetry chain
  • ground station and data transfer

Software

Software for engineers and for operators.

  • simulation and analysis tools
  • processing of measurements and records
  • operator and HMI applications
  • display and reporting

Testing

Planning, execution and processing of tests.

  • test matrices and procedures
  • instrumentation
  • laboratory and field testing
  • flight reconstruction from recorded data
  • test reports

Integration and documentation

Fitting a subassembly into the whole, and the documentation a system is produced from.

  • integration of a subassembly into an existing system
  • interface definition
  • support during qualification
  • technical documentation for series production

For an enquiry it is enough to describe the scope of work and the deadline. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before any technical data is exchanged.
