What we do

Services

We cover the whole chain — mechanics, electronics, software and cloud — so your solution doesn't have to be assembled from multiple vendors.

Industrial automation

Automation of processes and machinery: control cabinets, microcontroller and PLC control, sensors and actuators, safety functions, as well as modernization (retrofit) of existing plants.

Custom embedded systems

Development of purpose-built electronics and firmware: microcontroller platforms, GSM/LTE and GPS telemetry, industrial protocols, energy-efficient and solar-powered designs, over-the-air (OTA) updates in the field.

Guidance & Control software

Guidance, navigation and control (GNC) software for aircraft, drones and autonomous platforms: control and stabilization algorithms, sensor fusion, simulation and testing, autopilot and ground-station integration — plus a supporting product range.

Industrial equipment & machines

Design and manufacturing of purpose-built machines and devices for specific production needs — from surface-treatment machinery to special field equipment. From concept to commissioning.

Custom ERP systems

Business software tailored to your company: production and work-order tracking, warehouse and inventory, service records, reporting and integrations with your existing tools — an ERP that follows your process, not the other way around.

Cloud software solutions

IoT platforms for remote monitoring and control of devices: server infrastructure, web and mobile applications, real-time notifications and alarms, data history and analytics.

AI-driven development

Artificial intelligence in products and processes: AI agents and LLM integrations, computer vision for industrial inspection, predictive maintenance based on telemetry, and business-process automation — and we use AI tooling ourselves, so we build faster and more reliably.

How we work

A clear process, no surprises — at every step you know where the project stands.

  1. 01

    Consulting & analysis

    We get to know your problem, process and operating conditions. We define requirements, scope and budget.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concept and detailed engineering: mechanics, electronics, software and system architecture.

  3. 03

    Build & development

    Prototype and equipment manufacturing, firmware and software development, internal testing.

  4. 04

    Commissioning

    On-site installation and integration, user training, documentation handover.

  5. 05

    Support & maintenance

    Remote diagnostics, over-the-air (OTA) software updates and long-term support.
